How We Help

Project Riptide offers a scalable solution to microplastic pollution:

  • Active removal at high-concentration hotspots.
  • Real-time data streaming for scientific and policy insights.
  • Bio-inspired design that engages public awareness.

Clean today, learn for tomorrow — action plus data.

Our Solution

Our autonomous fish channel plastic into an AI-optimized pyrolysis system that:

  • Converts waste into bio‑crude, syngas, and char.
  • Uses syngas to power the process, creating a self-sustaining loop.
  • Enables continuous, intelligent cleanup.

From plastic to power: circular, continuous, intelligent cleanup.
