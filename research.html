Understanding the plastic problem — and the opportunity.

The Plastic Crisis

  • Plastic pollution — both macro and micro — severely degrades aquatic ecosystems.
  • Contaminates water bodies; harms marine life through ingestion and entanglement.
  • Disrupts food chains and biodiversity; poses risks to human health.

Every second counts before they become invisible pollutants.

Plastic Capture & Storage

  • Plastic accumulates in subtropical oceanic gyres, especially the Great Pacific Garbage Patch between Hawaii and California.
  • Estimated size: ~3× France; ~100 million kg across ~1.8 trillion pieces.
  • ~92% is macroplastic, currently fragmenting into microplastics.

Immediate cleanup must target macroplastics before they break down further.

Economic Value

Additional benefits

  • Reduced dependence on virgin fossil feedstocks.
  • Regional job creation in collection, sorting, and operations.
  • Avoidance of escalating waste management costs.

Turn plastic from liability to asset — profitably and sustainably.
